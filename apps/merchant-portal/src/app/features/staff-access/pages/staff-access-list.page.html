Role Permission Breakdown

OWNER

Full unrestricted control. Manages payouts, bank details, subscriptions, invoices, and staff invites.

OUTLET MANAGER

Runs outlet day-to-day. Manages catalogue pricing, pause/open schedules, stock levels, and refunds.

KITCHEN / CHEF

Focuses on food prep. Can accept orders, view kitchen notes, update prep status to Ready, and report stock-outs.

CASHIER

POS counter billing. Creates walk-in orders, accepts cash/UPI payments, prints thermal tokens, and hands over parcels.
